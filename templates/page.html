Readings are collected by the ESP32 and stored in Firebase. This dashboard displays the latest sensor output and its recent history.

Moisture is shown as the sensor’s raw capacitive reading. The values need calibration for the specific soil and planter before they can be interpreted as a percentage.

HOW IT WORKS 02 / 03

From soil
to signal.

The current version focuses on dependable sensing and logging. Automatic watering is the next step, once the readings are calibrated and the pump hardware is added.

01

Sense

A capacitive soil sensor connects to an ESP32 over I²C and provides a moisture reading plus sensor temperature.

ESP32 + STEMMA SOIL SENSOR
02

Log

Every ten minutes, the ESP32 timestamps the reading and sends it to Firebase. If a push fails, recent errors are saved locally for a retry.

FIREBASE REALTIME DATABASE
03

Water PLANNED

A small pump and driver circuit are planned. They will be added after the sensor data is calibrated, with control designed around clear moisture thresholds.
